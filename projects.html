Data Modeling & Engineering

Supply Chain Database Design & Data Modeling

Designed an 11-entity relational schema for a 180,000-row global supply chain dataset, taking it from a raw 53-column export through data preparation, business rules, and ER modeling in MySQL Workbench.

PythonMySQL WorkbenchData Modeling
  • Started from a raw 53-column dataset and led all data preparation in Python/Jupyter, resolving encoding errors and standardizing schema conventions before any modeling began.
  • Investigated ambiguous financial columns by reverse-engineering their calculation logic, resolving conflicts between overlapping fields before finalizing the schema.
  • Designed a many-to-many junction entity and a database-enforced weak entity relationship (UNIQUE constraint), introducing surrogate keys for entities lacking natural keys.
  • Authored complete business rules and a full data dictionary covering entity integrity, referential integrity, cardinality, and data type standards.
Result: Reduced the dataset from 53 to 42 columns with zero information loss, and delivered a complete ER model, business rules, and data dictionary ready for downstream database and warehouse implementation.

German Credit Risk Classification

Built and evaluated three classification models (logistic regression, decision tree, neural network) in R to predict credit risk under a cost-sensitive framework, as lead technical contributor on a graduate data mining case study.

RcaretCost-Sensitive ML
  • Designed a cost-sensitive evaluation framework reflecting a real 5:1 asymmetry between false-positive and true-positive outcomes; all three models were unprofitable at a standard threshold despite 69–75% accuracy.
  • Built a decile-wise lift chart to identify a profit-maximizing approval cutoff, approving only the top 40% of ranked applicants.
  • Applied stepwise feature selection, reducing the model from 30 to 19 predictors while improving both statistical fit and net profit.
  • Flagged fair-lending/ethical concerns in the dataset's demographic variables and incorporated them into the final recommendation.
Result: Converted a −4,600 DM loss into a ~+6,000 DM profit by optimizing the decision threshold, without changing the underlying model.

Reddit NLP Preprocessing Pipeline

Designed and built a two-stage text preprocessing pipeline (Python, pandas, spaCy, NLTK) that transformed ~720,000 raw, noisy Reddit records into ~507,000 clean, analysis-ready records for a graduate text-mining project.

PythonspaCyNLTK
  • Sequenced cheap filters ahead of expensive operations (language detection, lemmatization) for efficiency at 700K-record scale.
  • Identified a filler-word problem through dedicated validation and designed a second refinement stage without a full pipeline re-run.
  • Produced two purpose-built text representations per record: one for embedding/sentiment models, one for TF-IDF/clustering, instead of a single compromise output.
  • Built a dedicated validation stage (null audits, token frequency review, noise scans) that directly drove the pipeline's refinement.
Result: Delivered 74.3% record retention on comments and 35.8% on posts, with full documentation, data dictionaries, and reusable notebooks for team handoff.
Machine Learning

Reddit Sentiment Analysis

Applied and evaluated four sentiment analysis methods (TextBlob, VADER, Twitter-RoBERTa, multilingual BERT) across a 498,147-comment Reddit corpus as the sentiment analysis component owner on a graduate text-mining project.

PythonPyTorchHugging Face
  • Uncovered a 40-percentage-point disagreement in negative-sentiment classification across methods on identical text.
  • Diagnosed and corrected two hidden model biases: a dictionary-miss artifact inflating lexicon "neutral" labels by 4–50x, and a structurally biased star-rating conversion affecting 11.9% of transformer outputs.
  • Designed a bias-resistant, blind human-evaluation benchmark to select the most accurate model, rather than trusting any model's self-reported confidence.
  • Engineered a resumable, checkpointed GPU inference pipeline (Google Colab, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers) to process the full corpus with fault tolerance.
Result: Selected Twitter-RoBERTa as the most accurate model (70.0% accuracy, statistically significant over three alternatives) and combined it with topic modeling to identify a 27.8-point sentiment gap across discussion themes.
